Ecommerce Sites Are High-Value Targets

Online stores process thousands of transactions and customer records. Your website security directly impacts revenue and customer trust.

Ecommerce websites face constant automated attacks. Credit card skimmers, Magecart-style infections, and checkout page compromises are daily threats. We've audited online stores running Magento, Shopify, WooCommerce, and BigCommerce — each platform has its own security pitfalls. A single compromised checkout page can divert hundreds of credit card numbers to attackers before anyone notices.

Beyond payment data, ecommerce sites collect shipping addresses, phone numbers, purchase histories, and saved payment methods. This data has enormous value on the dark market. When your security headers are missing, your checkout pages lack proper encryption, or your CMS has known vulnerabilities, you're not just risking a breach — you're risking your entire business. Payment processors can terminate accounts, banks can freeze funds, and customers will never return.

Ecommerce Vulnerabilities We Find

💳

Checkout Security Gaps

Payment pages without proper SSL enforcement, missing security headers on checkout, and form submissions that don't properly encrypt customer data.

🛒

Platform-Specific Risks

WooCommerce plugin vulnerabilities, Magento exposure, Shopify theme issues, and platform-specific configuration flaws that create entry points.

👤

Customer Data Exposure

Customer accounts with weak password policies, profile pages exposing order history, and personal data leaking through APIs or directory listings.

📦

Order Information Leaks

Public order confirmation pages, exposed invoice PDFs, shipping labels with full addresses, and order tracking systems with authorization flaws.

🔌

Third-Party Script Risks

Payment gateways, analytics scripts, and marketing pixels that have excessive access to checkout data. Supply chain attacks via third-party JS.

🔍

Admin Panel Exposure

Magento admin panels at default paths, WordPress admin for WooCommerce stores, and login pages visible to attackers with weak protection.

PCI Considerations for Online Stores

What PCI Means for Your Website

If your website directly handles credit card data, PCI DSS requirements apply. Even if you use a third-party payment gateway, your website's security configuration affects your compliance scope. Our audit identifies website-level issues that could expand your PCI audit requirements or create compliance gaps.

Platform-Specific Audit Approaches

WooCommerce stores need WordPress-specific security checks — plugin vulnerabilities, theme exposure, and admin protection. Magento stores require version-specific vulnerability scanning and configuration review. Shopify stores need theme and app security review. Our Audit + Basic Pentest package adapts to your platform.

Customer Trust and Conversion

Security badges matter, but real security matters more. Customers can tell when a site has proper SSL, secure checkout, and no browser warnings. Our audit helps you fix the technical issues that silently kill conversion rates — missing HSTS, mixed content warnings, and expired certificates.

Multi-Store Operations

Many ecommerce businesses run multiple stores for different brands or regions. Each store needs individual review. Our Multi-Site Audit package covers your entire store portfolio with a consolidated dashboard.
