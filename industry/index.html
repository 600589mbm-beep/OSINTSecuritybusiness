Why Industry-Specific Audits Matter

A dental office faces different risks than an ecommerce store. A law firm has different compliance needs than a restaurant. Generic audits miss what matters most to your business.

Every industry collects different data, faces different regulatory requirements, and attracts different types of attackers. A generic website audit treats every site the same — checking the same boxes regardless of whether you're processing credit cards, handling patient data, or managing tenant applications. Industry-specific audits focus on the vulnerabilities that actually put your business at risk.

Our industry-specific audit pages explain the common vulnerabilities we find in each sector, why they matter, and how our audit process addresses them. Whether you need HIPAA-aware scanning for a dental practice, multi-site portfolio review for property managers, or white-label reports for an agency, we tailor our approach to your industry's needs.
