Tools We Use for Minneapolis Pentesting

Our penetration testing methodology uses professional-grade open-source tools that are trusted by security professionals worldwide. Nuclei provides our vulnerability scanning backbone, with templates covering thousands of known web vulnerabilities. OWASP ZAP helps us perform basic web application security testing. WPScan handles WordPress-specific security checks. SSLyze and testssl.sh verify your SSL/TLS configuration.

What sets our Minneapolis pentesting apart is not just the tools, but the transparency. You receive the raw output from every tool we run. No black-box reports where you have to trust our interpretation. You can see the Nuclei findings, the OWASP ZAP alerts, the SSLyze output, and the WPScan results for yourself. Combined with our plain-English summary and risk-ranked recommendations, you have everything needed to improve your website security.
