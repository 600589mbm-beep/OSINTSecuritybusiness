Professional Website Security Audits in Minneapolis, Minnesota

Comprehensive security checks for businesses across Minneapolis and St Paul.

Minneapolis, Minnesota is a major business hub in the Upper Midwest, with a diverse economy spanning healthcare, retail, manufacturing, financial services, and technology. From the skyscrapers of Downtown Minneapolis to the creative energy of Northeast Minneapolis, businesses across Hennepin County rely on their websites to generate leads, process transactions, and build trust with customers.

But Minnesota businesses face unique challenges. Whether you're running a medical practice in St Louis Park, a retail store in Bloomington, a professional service firm in Edina, or a restaurant in Uptown, your website needs regular security reviews to identify vulnerabilities before attackers do.

Our Minneapolis website security audit starts at $199 for a basic review. You get a plain-English report plus all the raw scan data.
