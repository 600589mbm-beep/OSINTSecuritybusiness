Minnesota Compliance and Security Needs

Minnesota businesses face unique regulatory and climate challenges that affect cybersecurity.

📋

Local Compliance Needs

Minnesota has strong data privacy protections and regulatory requirements that affect how businesses handle customer information. Healthcare providers must consider MN privacy laws alongside HIPAA. Financial services face state and federal overlap. Our audits help identify website-level compliance gaps that could create liability.

❄️

Cold Weather & Hosting

Minnesota winters are legendary, and extreme cold affects data center operations and hosting reliability. We check your SSL certificate expiration timing, CDN configuration, and server response patterns. A site that fails during a February blizzard doesn't just lose sales — it loses trust.

What Minnesota Small Businesses Get

Our small business cybersecurity audit is designed for the Minnesota business owner who wants straight answers about their website security. We check SSL/TLS configuration using SSLyze and testssl.sh, scan for vulnerabilities using Nuclei's extensive template library, review security headers, check for exposed files and directories, and assess CMS configuration for WordPress and other platforms.

But what really sets our service apart for Minnesota businesses is the raw data inclusion. You don't just get our interpretation — you get the actual scan outputs. The Nuclei results, the SSLyze report, the WPScan data if you're on WordPress, the httpx probe results — all included. This matters to Minnesota business owners who value transparency and want to understand the evidence behind security recommendations.
